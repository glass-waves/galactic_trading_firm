## c15_tom_day2_window — variation on a `pass-pretest` cell (catalog #17, matrix row 17, IDX = pass-pretest)

c11 (`c11_tom_day_of_cycle`) is the only tradable calendar cell currently at `pass-pretest`: long open→close on session 2 of the month clears the bar on QQQ (PF 1.73, 4/5 yrs) and SPY (PF 1.77, 5/5). The k=2 trade still carries open questions that the same data can answer cheaply. This cell keeps c11's rule and varies two things. First, the holding window: open→close, open→12:00 and 12:00→close, across k ∈ {2, 3, 1}. A sub-window carrying the whole effect would point to a morning or afternoon flow event rather than a day-level drift, and that changes how an engine window would be written. Second, the instrument class: the 4 mega-caps pooled and c12's 34-name pool, next to QQQ/SPY. c11 only tried index and sector ETFs. If the stock pools fail while QQQ/SPY pass, the effect is specific to index products. If they pass, the stock class is a much larger trade count for the same calendar rule. Pre-registered bar is unchanged (≥4/5 years, PF > 1.3), and a pass on a non-primary window or a stock pool also needs LOYO over its variant's grid to clear. The 5,000-draw random-session control runs for QQQ/SPY open→close, as in c11.
