## c18_tom_day2_prior — #17 turn-of-month, prior-session reversal confound

Catalog #17 / matrix row 17 (IDX `pass-pretest`). c11/c15 pass day +2 open->close; c16 (placebo, quarter
split) and c17 (weekday) address calendar and weekday confounds. Not yet tested: whether day +2 only
works after a down day +1, which would be short-term reversal rather than a month-position flow. The
cell splits day k in {2, 3} by the sign of the prior session's open->open return (known at the entry
open) and requires both strata to clear the bar on the 34-name pool; passing only after a down day
downgrades c11/c15/c16. Index strata are thin (~25-35 trades) and serve as controls.

## c19_tom_day2_sectors — #17 breadth across sector/size ETFs

Matrix row 17 lists SEC as `plausible`. c11 covered SPY, QQQ, IWM, XLK and SMH; XLF and XLE (already cached
in `data/bars_iex`) are untested. A broad turn-of-month flow should show on financials and energy; a
tech-drift artefact would not. Pre-registered grid: k in {1, 2, 3} x SPY/XLF/XLE/IWM/XLK; calendar-flow
reading requires k=2 to clear the bar on at least 4 of 5 ETFs including both XLF and XLE.
