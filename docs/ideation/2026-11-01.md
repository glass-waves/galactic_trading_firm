## c16_tom_day2_quarter — #17 turn-of-month, quarter-turn stratification + placebo

Catalog #17 / matrix row 17 (IDX `pass-pretest`). c11 and c15 found day +2 open->close passing on
SPY/QQQ (PF 1.7-1.8) and on the 34-name pool (PF 1.51, 4/5 yrs). The remaining open question is
causal: a pension/quarter-turn rebalancing story predicts a stronger effect in the first month of
a quarter (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct) and nothing mid-month, while a sample-drift story predicts similar
returns on any session. The cell pre-registers k=2 split by new-quarter vs other months, plus
placebo sessions k in {10, 11, 12}, on QQQ/SPY/mega-caps/34-name pool. If any placebo k clears the
bar on the same instrument, the family is downgraded before anyone spends an engine study on it.

## c17_tom_day2_weekday — #17 / #21 weekday-mix confound

Catalog #17 and #21 (Monday effect, decayed). Day +2 lands on a different weekday every month, so
a skewed Tue/Thu mix in this 5-year sample could look like a month-position edge. The cell splits
day +2 (and day +3 as a neighbour) into Mon/Fri vs Tue/Wed/Thu strata, verdict on the 4-name and
34-name pools (index strata are too thin: ~20-35 trades, kept as a control). Pass requires both
strata to clear the standard bar on the pool; one stratum carrying everything downgrades c11/c15.
