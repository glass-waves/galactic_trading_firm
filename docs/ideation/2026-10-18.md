## c13_letf_close_momentum — untested home cell (catalog #12, matrix row 12, IDX/LEV = home)

leveraged-ETF issuers must rebalance near the close in the direction of the day's move, and the required trade scales with the square of the return (Cheng-Madhavan 2009; Shum et al. 2016), so last-N-minute momentum should appear on large-move days even if it is absent on average. c08 (`c08_hedging_momentum`) showed plain last-30-min momentum has no gross edge on SPY/QQQ/IWM, but it never conditioned on move size. this cell trades the sign of the 09:30→(390−hold) return for the last `hold` minutes only when |return| ≥ thresh, with thresh∈{1.0,1.5,2.0}% and hold∈{30,15} min (6 grid points) on SPY, QQQ, IWM and XLK. standard bar (≥4/5 yrs, PF>1.3). the pre-registered pass also requires the large-move subset to beat the c08 ungated rows; a gross edge appearing only at the larger thresholds is the expected signature. the cache has no leveraged-ETF bars, so this uses the underlying indices as the proxy, and the dealer-gamma leg (needs options OI) is deliberately excluded. expect few trades per year at the 2% threshold, so a `fragile` or thin-sample verdict is likely.
