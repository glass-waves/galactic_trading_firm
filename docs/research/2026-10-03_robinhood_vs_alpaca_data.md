# Robinhood agentic trading vs. Alpaca market data (2026-10-03)

Owner question: Robinhood just launched an agentic connection; does its market data beat Alpaca
for our use case (volume-accurate 1-minute bars, 4-5 names + SPY, intraday paper trading)?
Web research only, as of 2026-10-03. Everything below is attributed; items I could not verify
independently are marked **(unverified)**.

## Findings, by topic

**1. What Robinhood launched.** Robinhood shipped an official MCP ("Model Context Protocol") server — `agent.robinhood.com/mcp/trading` — on 2026-05-27, one of the first brokerage-run MCP endpoints, initially equities-only, in beta for eligible US customers ([TechCrunch, 2026-05-27](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/robinhood-now-lets-your-ai-agents-trade-stocks/); [Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-is-now-open-to-agents/)). It expanded to crypto on 2026-07-20 ([Genfinity, 2026-07-21](https://genfinity.io/2026/07/21/robinhood-agentic-trading-crypto-ai-agents/); [Robinhood on X](https://x.com/RobinhoodApp/status/2072716802145014050)). Separately, at the HOOD Summit (announced ~2026-09-29/30) Robinhood unveiled a broader in-app "Robinhood Agents" product (works with OpenAI/Anthropic models) plus weekend trading hours — distinct from **Cortex**, which Robinhood describes as a copilot ("suggests, explains, places the orders you request") rather than an autonomous trader ([CNBC, 2026-09-30](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/robinhood-unveils-weekend-hours-ai-agents-to-allow-users-to-trade-nonstop.html); [CoinDesk, 2026-09-29](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/29/robinhood-adds-ai-agents-perps-and-weekend-trading-in-push-to-win-active-traders); [AlgoAlpha blog on Cortex](https://algoalpha.co/blog/robinhood-cortex-review)).

Per Robinhood's own support article, the agent gets read access to "all your Robinhood accounts... positions and balances... transactions, including order history," can place orders, but **only inside a separate, dedicated Agentic account** you fund yourself; your primary investing account must be "in good standing" to open one ([Robinhood support: Agentic Trading Overview](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/agentic-trading-overview/)). No Gold requirement or waitlist is stated in what I could read; US-customer scope is implied, not stated as a hard restriction. Market-data capability described there is "market data tools" for research/analysis (e.g. "why is X up today"), not a documented bar/tick export — I found no evidence the MCP exposes a raw OHLCV+volume stream suitable for a VPIN-style indicator.

**2. Market data specifics.** Robinhood's general real-time pricing (outside the new MCP) is single-venue/last-sale based: Nasdaq Last Sale during regular hours, an ATS feed overnight, surfaced as a "consolidated... last sale, best bid, best ask" figure on the trade-entry screen only ([Robinhood support: Using Market Data](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/360025308191/using-market-data/)). That page does not mention historical bars or volume at all. Independent write-ups note the (unofficial, reverse-engineered) consumer API has **no websocket** — you must poll — and only coarse historical granularity (5/10/30-min, day/week bars; day/week/year/5yr/all ranges), plus a stated-but-app-contradicting ToS clause allowing up to a 20-minute quote delay ([AlgoTrading101](https://algotrading101.com/learn/robinhood-api-guide/); [paperswithbacktest.com](https://paperswithbacktest.com/datasets/robinhood-api-guide) — lower-confidence secondary sources, **unverified against Robinhood's own docs**). Robinhood Gold ($5/mo, or $50/yr) adds Nasdaq TotalView Level 2 — a Nasdaq-only order book, not multi-venue consolidated depth, and I found no evidence it's exposed through the MCP or any documented API ([FinanceBuzz](https://financebuzz.com/robinhood-gold-worth-it); [Robinhood support: Level II Market Data](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/level-ii-market-data/)). Net: nothing found indicates Robinhood exposes a volume-bearing, consolidated, real-time 1-minute bar feed via any API, agentic or otherwise.

**3. Paper trading.** Robinhood has **no paper/simulated trading account**, via app or API — confirmed by multiple independent sources as of September 2026 ([chartmini.com](https://chartmini.com/blog/does-robinhood-have-paper-trading); [investingsimple.com](https://www.investingsimple.com/can-you-paper-trade-with-robinhood/)). "Simulated Returns" is an options payoff-scenario tool, not a funded practice account. The new Agentic account is explicitly real money, just segregated from your main portfolio.

**4. Alpaca today.** Confirmed directly from Alpaca's docs: Basic (free) = IEX feed only, 200 req/min REST, 30-symbol websocket cap, SIP historical data withheld for the most recent 15 minutes; Algo Trader Plus ($99/mo) = full consolidated SIP across all US exchanges, 10,000 req/min, unlimited websocket symbols, no 15-minute historical restriction, history back to 2016 on both tiers ([docs.alpaca.markets, About Market Data API](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api)). No evidence of a 2026 price change; $99/mo matches our existing context and secondary sources ([TradingDataCompare](https://tradingdatacompare.com/providers/alpaca/)).

**5. Other options (one line each).**
- **Polygon/Massive** (rebranded 2026): consolidated SIP-sourced minute aggregates with volume, websocket + flat files; "Stocks Advanced" real-time is **$199/mo** individual ([massive.com docs](https://massive.com/docs/websocket/stocks/aggregates-per-minute); [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Polygon.io)).
- **Databento**: consolidated NBBO/Equities feeds, ohlcv-1m aggregates, no exchange license fees; Standard plan **$199/mo** (unlimited, 7yr OHLCV history); a cheaper "Equities Mini" consolidated tier is mentioned but its exact price I could not confirm **(unverified)** ([databento.com/equities](https://databento.com/equities)).
- **Tradier**: Pro plan **$10/mo** bundles real-time quotes, full REST/WS API, and a free developer sandbox with paper trading (sandbox lacks delayed *streaming*, only REST); consolidation level of its real-time feed not independently confirmed **(unverified)** ([tradier.com/individuals/pricing](https://tradier.com/individuals/pricing); [docs.tradier.com](https://docs.tradier.com/docs/getting-started)).
- **Public.com**: trading API is **free**, "Get bars v2" returns OHLCV for up to 5 years, real-time quotes powered by Xignite (built on NASDAQ Last Sale/Basic per Xignite's own product pages) — likely single-venue/Nasdaq-weighted rather than full CTA/UTP consolidated, **not confirmed either way** ([public.com/api/docs/changelog](https://public.com/api/docs/changelog); [xignite.com/stock-and-etf-data](https://www.xignite.com/stock-and-etf-data/)).
- **Schwab Trader API / thinkorswim**: **free** with a funded Schwab account; CHART stream gives minute OHLCV, Level 1 streaming is broker-grade (NBBO-driven); exact consolidation guarantee not independently re-verified **(unverified)** ([schwab-py docs](https://schwab-py.readthedocs.io/en/latest/streaming.html); [Grokipedia: Schwab Trader API](https://grokipedia.com/page/Schwab_Trader_API)).
- **IBKR**: historical 1-min bars available via TWS API; live `reqRealTimeBars` is 5-second only (would need to roll up to 1-min); market-data add-ons (e.g. NYSE/Nasdaq consolidated tape bundles) are billed monthly, typically a few dollars each and often waived against commissions — exact current price **not confirmed** ([IBKR Campus: Requesting Market Data](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/campus/trading-lessons/requesting-market-data/); [TWS API docs: Realtime Bars](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/realtime_bars.html)).

## Recommendation for our use case

Robinhood's new agentic MCP does **not** solve our problem. It has no paper trading (any bot there trades real money immediately), no documented websocket or OHLCV+volume export, and its general market-data pipeline looks single-venue (Nasdaq Last Sale/Basic), not the CTA/UTP consolidated tape our VPIN indicator needs. It's built for "research + place a trade," not for feeding a tick loop. Do not route data through it; the owner's existing Robinhood account is real-money and shouldn't be wired into an unattended bot at all without an explicit, separate decision.

Stay on Alpaca, and treat **Algo Trader Plus ($99/mo)** as the straightforward fix once VPIN accuracy is worth paying for: it's the same API/broker we already integrate, confirmed full SIP, no code changes beyond the subscription. If cost is the blocker, **Tradier Pro ($10/mo)** is the one alternative worth a trial specifically because it bundles a free paper-trading sandbox with its real-time feed — but its consolidation level is unverified, so pull a day of its 1-min bars and diff volume against a known-SIP source (e.g. a short Algo Trader Plus trial) before trusting it for VPIN. Schwab's free-with-account API is attractive on price but would mean opening/linking a new account and the same unverified-consolidation caveat applies.
