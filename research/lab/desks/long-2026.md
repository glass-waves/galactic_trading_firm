# desk long-2026 — a long side on a 1h that has a direction (2026-11-01)

Agent `long-2026`. 37 distinct specs / ~40 ledger runs (budget ≈ 60). No holdout dates touched, no engine/DB/pipeline edits, no commits.
Everything is reproducible: `python3 research/lab/patches/long-2026/make_cells.py` (block A + scaffold), then
`make_cells.py research/lab/patches/long-2026/blocks_ce.py` (blocks C/E) and `make_cells.py research/lab/patches/long-2026/blocks_q.py`
(blocks F, P, Q, R, V, W, K — it also re-creates F/P from `blocks_fp.py` byte-identically). Specs: `research/lab/specs/long-2026/`.
Run artifacts: `research/lab/runs/long-2026__<cell>.<window>.{json,trades.csv}`.

## TL;DR

1. **The morning long is dead, even with a 1h that has a direction.** Row 12's three long windows re-enabled lose −4,632 / 2,669 / PF 0.86 on
   train. Adding the directional 1h (standard supertrend bullish, signed ADX ≥ 0.2/0.4) reaches at best PF 0.88 on train (`a3`). The full
   v18-filter mirror on top (`f1`: + VPIN) is PF 1.01 on train, **+327 / 339 / 1.09 on 2023_25h1**, 2022 −644 on the long side. Every SPY/gap/clock
   regime filter on the 2024h2–25h1 screen keeps PF 0.60–0.83. Answer to the coordinator's question: **yes, the directional-1h morning longs are flat
   on 2023_25h1.** I switched to the afternoon-volatility angle (meta desk note) about one third of the way through the budget.
2. **The afternoon high-volatility breakout long on the mega-caps is the one long that holds up on train.** This is the noise-area window from row 23
   (cand_48) run on AAPL AMZN NVDA MSFT, long side only. Its high-vol gate, `noise.day_move_pct`, is the name's own 14-session average daily move.
   The gate had to be rescaled for single names: 0.7 is QQQ's value, and on the mega-caps that is "every day" (PF 0.95 on the long side once
   2025-04-09 is excluded). With the gate at ≥ 1.5 the long side alone makes +1,510 / 274 / 1.35. Two filters each help, both by removing losing days
   (mostly 2022 days against the trend): the **VPIN floor 0.217** (`r3`: +1,360 / 159 / **1.66**, 4/4 years, worst day −158) and **the directional 1h
   bullish** (`r4`: +1,532 / 210 / 1.52, sign-flip p 0.035, 4/4 years). Both together (`w1`): +1,096 / 122 / 1.74, worst day −94.
3. **Its short twin loses every year.** The mirrored afternoon window with the same VPIN floor (`t1`) is −1,313 / 152 / 0.58. So the edge is
   directional: buy upside breaks on volatile days. It is not "volatile afternoons pay".
4. **The single biggest caveat is 2025-04-09** (the tariff-pause rally), worth +344 … +921 depending on the cell. Without April 2025 the
   nominees are PF 1.37 (`r3`), 1.42 (`v1`) and 1.54 (`w1`) on train, and PF 1.26–1.38 on 2023_25h1. 2025h1 without April is about zero on 4–9 trades.
   The book is thin: 2.9–3.8 trades a month.
5. **The form is its own book, not added windows.** Added to row 12 as a window, v18's breakeven 0.5 % and 5m ATR×7 trail act on the afternoon
   positions too. They cut the long side from +1,096 to +697 (`k3`), and with no VPIN/1h filter from +1,510 to +304 (`k1`). No exit action except
   `vwap_stop` can be scoped to one window (see engine proposal). Run as a second book next to v18, the daily correlation is −0.00 to −0.02.
   v18 + `r3` gives train **+2,539 / 483 / PF 1.44**, P(diff ≤ 0) 0.048, combined sign-flip p 0.014.

What this screen says:
- **The directional 1h is not the missing piece for morning longs.** A bullish 1h barely moves PF (0.67 → 0.68). A strong signed trend (ADX ≥ 0.4)
  cuts volume by 70 % and reaches 0.84. The long-side losses sit in strong core (PF 0.71–0.73 on hundreds of trades), ScoreExit (−1,000 on ~20 trades)
  and HardStop.
- **SPY above its open or above the prior close makes longs worse** (0.60, 0.64). The trades they remove are also losers (`diff.py`: removed long
  trades PF 0.88 / 0.92), so neither side of the SPY split works. Morning mean reversion beats chasing.
- **The SPY-flat band is a short-side filter.** Shorts lose it and fall 0.98 → 0.71; longs do not move (0.68 → 0.69). **The VPIN floor is not
  short-only**: longs go 0.68 → 0.73, and NVDA longs turn positive (+143 / 92 / 1.12). Shorts without it are +65 vs −21 on this window, but 5-year
  history says it lifts shorts.
- **The open (09:30–10:00) is the worst time to buy** (0.65). 10:00–11:30 is the least bad (0.75), the mirror of the short book.
- **NVDA is consistently the least-bad long name.** AAPL is the worst in the morning, which contradicts HISTORY §E's "NVDA/AAPL first".
- Side finding for the indicators desk: `a4` (dir1h in the 1h sum) **lifts v18's shorts on this window to +482 / 154 / 1.28** (vs +59 / 97), mostly
  through strong core short (1h ≤ −0.4 becomes reachable).

### mechanism (trade diffs, ticker-day level)

- q1 → r3 (VPIN): removes 114 ticker-days worth **−1,085 / PF 0.56** (76 of them in 2022). On the 158 kept days, entries move to a later 30-min
  decision point where VPIN clears, and those days earn +1,360 instead of +2,596. The filter picks days well but enters late. An engine idea, not
  tested: latch the day once VPIN clears and take the first later breakout.
- q1 → r4 (1h bullish): removes 63 ticker-days worth **−791 / PF 0.47** (36 in 2022: afternoon breakouts against a bearish 1h). The same delay cost
  applies (+2,302 → +1,532 on kept days).
- The directional 1h does real work here and not in the morning. In the afternoon it is a trend filter on a momentum entry. In the morning the long
  windows are already trend-following on 5m, and the 1h adds nothing.
- Exits (`q1`): 254 SessionClose +3,356, 20 HardStop −1,845. The trade is "buy the break, hold to the close". Earlier QQQ work found a VWAP stop

## dead ends (with reasons)

- Morning long windows under any 1h definition: legacy, standard-supertrend bullish, + signed ADX 0.2/0.4, or dir1h in the sum (`a0`–`a4`).
- SPY above open / above prior close / ticker gap-up as a long regime (`c1`–`c3`). They make longs worse. The complements are also negative.
- The SPY-flat band on longs (`c4`): short-side only.
- Clocks for morning longs (`e1`–`e3`): 10:00–11:30 is least bad (0.75), still a loss.
- NVDA/AAPL morning long with VPIN and the directional 1h (`f1` subset, `f4`): positive 2023_25h1, loses 2022 every way.
- Row 23 as-is on the mega-caps (`p1`): the QQQ day_move 0.7 gate is "always on" for single names. The afternoon shorts (`p1`–`p3`, `t1`) lose every year.
- SPY confirmation on the afternoon long (`r1`, `r2`): slightly worse than none. The breakout already implies the up-tape.

## engine proposals (not implemented)

1. **Scope for breakeven / ATR-trail / hard-stop actions** (like `vwap_stop.scope_min_max_hold_ms`, or a `scope_max_max_hold_ms` to exclude windows
   with a long max-hold override). This would let the afternoon long live inside the promoted config without v18's morning exits gutting it
   (`k1` vs `q1`: +304 vs +1,510).
2. **A relative volatility key on `noise_area`**, e.g. `day_move_ratio` = 14-session day move ÷ 60-session median. The high-vol gate would then mean the
   same thing for NVDA and AAPL.
3. **A day latch for entry filters** (once VPIN ≥ x has been seen today, allow the next breakout). This would recover the late-entry cost measured above.

## nominations (≤ 3) — best of the 21 afternoon-angle cells (`p*`, `q*`, `r*`, `v*`, `w*`, `t1`, `k*`); best of 37 desk specs overall

### N1 — `research/lab/specs/long-2026/r3_q1_vpin.json` (afternoon high-vol breakout long + VPIN floor)

Why: highest PF of the un-stacked single-filter cells. It uses v18's existing VPIN value (0.217), not a fitted one. The ±1 neighbourhood is a
plateau: VPIN 0.18 / 0.217 / 0.26 gives PF 1.65 / 1.66 / 1.98, and day_move 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 gives 1.39 / 1.66 / 2.14 (v4: +1,567 / 105, ex-April PF 1.74). PF rises with the gate as volume falls, so this is a quality/volume dial with no cliff. I chose 1.5 over 1.75 for volume (159 vs 105 trades). 4/4 calendar years positive.
Worst day −158, dd −287. Its short twin loses every year, which says the edge is directional. Ex-April-2025 train PF 1.37.
Holdout expectation (written before any reveal): roughly 3–4 trades a month, so **45–65 trades** on 2025-07-01 → 2026-10-30, mostly NVDA, plus AAPL
and MSFT only in volatile spells. A bull tape has few high-vol days, so the count may come in below that. **Expected PF 1.15–1.45, P&L +100…+450**.
I'd put about a 30 % chance on PF < 1.0, given ~60 trades and the April-2025 dependence in the train record. Standalone it cannot meet test 1 (≥ 150
trades). As a second book next to v18, the expected target-window combined PF is ≈ 1.35–1.5 on ~200 trades.

### N2 — `research/lab/specs/long-2026/w1_q1_vpin_bull1h.json` (N1 + directional 1h bullish)
